WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla.

The organization works towards creating positive social change by addressing important community needs and supporting people in different areas.

Its initiatives focus on education, community support, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental conservation and skill development.

Our Mission

To encourage people to participate in social initiatives and work together towards a better, more inclusive and sustainable society.
